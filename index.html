Good questions

Things people ask before they sign

Will callers know it's an AI?

The agent identifies itself as an AI assistant at the start of every call — it's good practice and legally required in several states. The voice is natural enough that the conversation feels easy, and a real person is one sentence away whenever the caller wants one.

How fast can my agent go live?

Most agents are built, tested, and live within 7 business days. Simple single-workflow setups can be faster; heavily customized multilingual flows with deep CRM logic take a little longer.

Which languages do you actually support?

30+ languages and regional dialects, including English, Spanish (LatAm & Iberian), Hindi, Mandarin, Cantonese, Arabic, French, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Korean, Tagalog and more. If yours isn't listed, ask — coverage is expanding constantly.

Does it connect to my CRM?

Yes. Native HubSpot and Salesforce, plus GoHighLevel and anything reachable through Zapier. Every call writes transcripts, sentiment, and outcomes back to your records automatically.

Is it compliant?

We handle TCPA do-not-call scrubbing, consent language, and recording disclosures, and operate on SOC 2 infrastructure. We'll also recommend you run your specific use case past your own counsel before launch.

Who owns and stores my data? Is it private?

You own it, completely. Every call, transcript, and contact lives in your own CRM and systems. Your data stays private — we never store, sell, or share your customer data, and we don't keep standing access to it. The agent simply reads from and writes to the systems you already control.

Do I need technical staff to run it?

No. It's a done-for-you managed service. We build, integrate, and maintain the agent. You just watch the dashboard and answer the warm leads it hands you.
